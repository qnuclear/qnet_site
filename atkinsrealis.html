About AtkinsRéalis

AtkinsRéalis is a world-class engineering services and nuclear organization headquartered in Montréal. It is the steward of CANDU reactor technology and a global leader in nuclear power, from new build and life extension to small modular reactors and decommissioning.

AtkinsRéalis connects people, data and technology to transform the world's infrastructure and energy systems.

What Our Inaugural Partnership Makes Possible

As QNET's first sponsor, AtkinsRéalis believed in the team before we had a track record. Because QNET isn't eligible for Queen's University funding until a year after ratification, their early support gave us the footing to start building, teaching, and growing from day one.

A Foundation to Grow

Their early commitment gave QNET the stability to turn a newly ratified club into a working design team, and set the direction for what comes next: new projects, more members, and a lasting home for nuclear engineering at Queen's.

QNET School

Their support helps run QNET School, our hands-on nuclear education program. It funds certifications, guest lectures from industry professionals, tours of facilities like Port Hope and the RMTL, and tools like cloud chambers and Geiger counters that let members see radiation physics in action.

Real Builds, Real Skills

Their partnership helps turn designs into hardware, including our 2026–2027 flagship CANDU® Cooling System. Members gain real experience designing, building, and testing, along with the skills that prepare them for careers in the Canadian nuclear industry.
